# H3 prompt construction in Frame Studio

Use the input mode and labels supplied by the task. The English narration of the prompt is separate from the original language of spoken content and visible signs. Work from the supplied shot only. The host owns the uploaded picture order, clip duration, original dialogue and speaker attribution. Insert the literal dialogue placeholder only where requested by the tool schema. Describe the moment visually before it and continue the approved action after it.

Choose concrete on-screen facts. Establish frame size and point of view, which subject is visible where, the costume established by the selected picture, what changes over time, and how the camera moves. Use the existing lighting and setting. Do not invent extra shots, soundtrack, motives, or performances. Time cues must agree with the specified duration. Leave silent scenes free of speech and song. Preserve spoken language, names and visible text. The application inserts original lines and applies the selected mode's final section ordering and keyframe alignment.

Use the mode-specific local reference. For a text or keyframe request, follow the three sections in h3-keyframes-en.md. For reusable media, follow the six sections and binding rules in h3-reference-en.md. These are Frame Studio's prompt rules for its actual supported workflow.
